Withdraw SOL from your old Alpha.art offers

Unofficial, open-source page, not affiliated with Alpha.art. When you made an offer on Alpha.art, its SOL was locked in a separate on-chain account. The Alpha.art website is gone, but the program still lets the bidder cancel these offers. This page finds every open offer made by your wallet and sends the same Cancel Bid instruction. The SOL goes to your own wallet, and you sign the transactions yourself.

What exactly you sign

Only instructions of the Alpha.art program HZaWndaNWHFDd9Dhk5pqUUtsmoBCqzb1MLu3NAh1VX6B, Cancel Bid (data 04), one per offer, up to 10 per transaction. Accounts are just your wallet (signer, receives the SOL) and the offer account (closed). Each one closes an offer account that your wallet created and sends its full balance to your wallet. There are no token approvals and no transfers to anyone else. Network fee is 0.000005 SOL per transaction. Your wallet shows its own simulation before you sign.

Source code and how to verify it: see the repository this page is served from.
