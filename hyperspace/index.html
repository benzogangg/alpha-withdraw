What exactly you sign

One instruction of the Hyperspace program HYPERfwdTjyJ2SCaKHmpF2MtrXqWxrsotYDsTrshHWq8, Withdraw (b712469c946da122). It moves the full balance of your escrow ["hyperspace", auction house, your wallet] to your own wallet. There are no token approvals and no transfers to anyone else. Network fee is 0.000005 SOL. Your wallet shows its own simulation before you sign.
